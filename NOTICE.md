# Reuse and third-party data

These materials are made publicly accessible for scientific review and reproducibility. Repository visibility alone is not an open-source license. No additional software license or blanket data-reuse grant has been selected on behalf of the authors.

Public monitoring records retain the terms, attribution requirements, and limitations of the originating agencies. Cite EPA, Washington State Department of Health, and USGS as appropriate. The released analysis tables do not imply agency endorsement.

Contact the repository owner through GitHub for reuse questions not covered by the original data-source terms. A future license decision should be recorded explicitly and must not purport to relicense third-party material.
