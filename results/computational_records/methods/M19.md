## Text S19. Marginal-Preserving Independence Reference

The source-water comparison was extended after inspection of the concentration-floor results. Exactly the 20,162 locations and fixed 59-stratum weights from Text S18 were retained. For each source-water category within each jurisdiction-by-system-size cell, the empirical reporting probability of every analyte was calculated at native limits and floors of 5, 10, 20, and 40 ng/L. The reference joint distribution treated the analytes as independent Bernoulli variables with these probabilities. Its count distribution is Poisson-binomial.<sup>17</sup> For a cell with probabilities $p_j$, its probability of at least two reports equals one minus the probabilities of zero and one report. A recursive calculation avoided division by 1 − $p_j$ and remained defined when an analyte was reported in every record.

The reference preserves the observed cell-specific analyte frequencies. It is also the exact expectation under independent permutations of the analyte columns within each cell. Those permutations preserve the full analyte-specific concentration distributions and censoring masks, but not the within-sample combination. The observed and reference probabilities were standardized using the same weights. The panel-induced change was calculated for both before taking their difference. This reference does not preserve all location-level covariates or the clustering of different analytes within PWSs. Its discrepancy from observed reporting can include heterogeneity within cells and must not be interpreted as a causal dependence parameter or a source-apportionment result.

Both components were recomputed jointly in 2,000 PWS-cluster resamples within fixed jurisdictions. One draw omitted an entire source-by-stratum support cell and was excluded from all paired statistics, leaving 1,999 estimable draws. Percentile intervals describe sampling variation conditional on estimable fixed-support resamples and the inspected archive. They do not incorporate analytical measurement error, support selection, or the prior development of these exploratory questions. The computational archive retains the protocol and its implementation amendment.

At native limits, the panel-induced source contrast was 4.640 percentage points in the observations and 7.310 in the independence reference; observed minus reference was −2.669 points (95% interval, −3.545 to −1.650). At a common 20 ng/L cutoff, the corresponding values were 0.134, 0.055, and 0.080 points, with the difference interval spanning zero. All five conditions are retained in Table S36 and Figure S5. At native limits, preserving the observed within-sample arrangement yielded a smaller panel effect than the independence reference. This direction was not stable across concentration floors.

Full-polynomial convolution and 250 independent within-cell concentration permutations checked the reference calculation. Exact estimates, permutation means, and Monte Carlo errors are retained in the computational verification record. Those errors assess the numerical reference, not uncertainty in an environmental effect.
