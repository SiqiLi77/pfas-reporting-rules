## Text S3. Identification Limits and Sharp Bounds under Fixed Marginal Probabilities

An old-cycle result reported as below the MRL is compatible with either a modeled zero component or a positive concentration below that reporting level. Let $B=1$ denote an archived below-MRL record. A hurdle representation makes this ambiguity explicit:

\[
P(B=1\mid x)=\pi_0(x)+[1-\pi_0(x)]F_+(L\mid x),
\]

where $\pi_0(x)$ is structural-zero probability and $F_+$ is the positive-concentration distribution. The observed below-MRL probability does not separately identify these components. This structural-zero representation is an identification example, not the native-MRL reporting hurdle fitted in Text S4.

The identification example used 200,000 synthetic records in two observation-equivalent latent worlds. The shared-sub-MRL and independently dispersed worlds had a bitwise-identical archived binary reporting matrix and the same analyte-specific low-threshold prevalences, ranging from 2.70% to 2.77%, but $P(K_6\ge2)$ was 1.900% and 0.538%, respectively. These worlds were used only to demonstrate the identification problem. UCMR 5 masking labels came from modern measurements, and UCMR 3 below-MRL records were not relabeled.

For each outer-test location and query threshold, let $p=(p_1,\ldots,p_6)$ be the six supplied marginal reporting probabilities and $q_y$ the probability assigned to state $y\in\{0,1\}^6$. The sharp bounds were obtained by solving

\[
\min/\max\ \sum_{y:I(\sum_j y_j\ge2)=1}q_y
\]

subject to nonnegative state probabilities that sum to one and reproduce each supplied analyte-specific marginal probability. For each subset $A$ containing at least two of the six analytes, $b_A$ was the sum of the marginal probabilities in $A$, minus 1, divided by $|A|-1$. The sharp lower bound was the greater of zero and the largest $b_A$ over these subsets. The upper bound was

\[
\min\left\{1,\frac{\sum_jp_j}{2},\sum_jp_j-\max_jp_j\right\}.
\]

The implementation was checked against a general 64-state linear-program solver. Across 338,496 equally weighted location–threshold records, the mean and median bound widths were 0.03680 and 0.01461 (Table S6). These conditional partial-identification bounds exclude sampling uncertainty and uncertainty in the supplied marginal probabilities. The synthetic examples above are not UCMR estimates; Table S6 summarizes the empirical bounds.
