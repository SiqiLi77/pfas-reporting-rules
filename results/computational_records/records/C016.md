January models are evaluated without refitting or recalibration on records first appearing in the final archive, which need not have been collected later. The continuous-model Brier advantage is −0.000054 (95% PWS-bootstrap interval, −0.000199 to 0.000072); negative values favor endpoint-specific models.

Joint scores weight the 69 events and 16 thresholds equally. Relative to independence, shared shock and Gaussian reduced pattern NLL by 13.81% and 13.32% and count Brier score by 21.03% and 20.79%; their binary Brier scores were 8.97% and 7.01% higher.
